# Six new rungs — names, characters and avatar prompts

Six ratings were asked for, to make the climb feel less like a jump for the
players who are still learning it: **950, 1050, 1150** for a beginner, and
**1350, 1450, 1600** for someone who has settled in. Two candidates each,
either of which can be commissioned as-is.

`doc/history/Avatars.md` is the source for the house style and for what the
twenty existing avatars are. The roster order and every rating in it are still
exactly what `bot.dart` ships — what changed since it was written is Vireo's and
Finch's *blurbs* (D314, D315), not their places. So the progression that
document describes is the one these six have to slot into without breaking:

> Cute → Animal → Guardian → Mythical → Godlike

Every prompt below ends with the same tail the existing twenty use, so the set
stays one commission rather than two.

---

## 950 — the last free rung

Between **Clover** (900, lucky rabbit with a four-leaf clover) and **Bramble**
(1000, hedgehog with berry-covered spines). Novice tier, and **free**: the free
set is everything rated 1000 or under, so this one costs nothing and is the
last opponent a new player meets before the ladder starts charging. That makes
it the most valuable of the six — a whole extra step of "I can beat this"
before the first price tag.

Still firmly in *Cute*. Smaller and softer than the hedgehog it leads to.

**Option A — Sorrel**
A leggy young hare the colour of autumn bracken, ears too big for it, sitting
in a patch of wood sorrel. Curious rather than brave.

> Young russet hare spirit with oversized ears, sitting among wood sorrel and
> clover, warm amber and green palette, soft dappled forest light, whimsical
> fantasy illustration, soft painterly style, circular portrait, highly
> detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Hazel**
A dormouse curled around a hazelnut twice its size, sleepy-eyed, in a nest of
gold leaves. The most harmless thing on the ladder that can still beat you.

> Tiny dormouse forest spirit hugging a hazelnut, sleepy golden eyes, nest of
> autumn leaves, warm honey and green palette, cozy woodland fantasy
> illustration, soft painterly style, circular portrait, highly detailed,
> 400x400 avatar

## 1050 — the first paid step after Bramble

Between **Bramble** (1000, hedgehog) and **Willow** (1100, young tree spirit).
Casual tier. This is the rung that has to carry a player over the line from
*Animal* into *Guardian*, so it should be an animal that already looks like it
is guarding something.

**Option A — Bracken**
A fern-frond creature, half plant and half beast, unfurling. Green on green,
with the coiled crozier of a new fern for a tail.

> Small woodland guardian made of unfurling fern fronds, coiled fiddlehead
> tail, mossy green palette, alert bright eyes, forest floor setting,
> whimsical fantasy illustration, soft painterly style, circular portrait,
> highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Juniper**
A young badger in a cloak of juniper needles, blue berries at the collar,
striped face set in a frown. Small, and takes itself completely seriously.

> Young badger forest guardian wearing a cloak of juniper needles and blue
> berries, striped face, determined expression, deep green and slate palette,
> woodland fantasy illustration, soft painterly style, circular portrait,
> highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

## 1150 — between the sapling and the songbird

Between **Willow** (1100, young willow tree spirit) and **Vireo** (1200, wise
songbird elder). Casual tier. The trap here is drawing a second Willow. The
brief for both options is therefore **older**, not merely leafier: Willow is a
sapling with a face, this is a tree that has been standing a while.

**Option A — Rowan**
A rowan spirit in autumn, bark like weathered silver, a crown of red berries,
holding a staff cut from its own branch. Where Willow is a season old, Rowan
has seen a few.

> Autumn rowan tree spirit, weathered silver bark skin, crown of red rowan
> berries, carved branch staff, amber and crimson palette, standing in low
> golden light, magical woodland guardian, whimsical fantasy illustration,
> soft painterly style, circular portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Aspen**
An aspen spirit of white bark and coin-shaped leaves that never stop moving,
pale and quick, the whole figure caught mid-shiver in the wind.

> Aspen tree spirit with white bark skin and trembling coin-shaped leaves,
> pale silver-green palette, hair and leaves streaming in wind, luminous
> quiet eyes, magical woodland guardian, whimsical fantasy illustration, soft
> painterly style, circular portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

## 1350 — the bridge out of Casual

Between **Kestrel** (1300, hunter bird, the top of Casual) and **Marlin**
(1400, river hunter, the start of Club). The one place on the ladder where the
tier label changes, so this avatar is doing the most work of the six: it has to
be a step up from a bird of prey *and* point at a creature of water.

Recommended tier: **Casual**, so a player gets one more rung before the label
above the panel changes. Club is defensible instead, if the intent is that the
harder tier should start sooner.

**Option A — Osprey** *(recommended)*
A fish hawk, wings back, talons out, half a wingspan above dark water. It is
literally the animal between Kestrel and Marlin, and the composition says so.

> Noble osprey fish hawk in a dive, white and dark brown plumage, talons
> extended over dark river water, spray and low mist, fierce amber eyes,
> forest river setting, fantasy portrait, painterly game avatar, circular
> portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Heron**
Grey-blue, motionless, standing in shallow water among reeds. Patience drawn
as a threat — the opposite temperament to Kestrel and just as dangerous.

> Tall grey-blue heron spirit standing motionless in still reed water, sharp
> beak, unblinking pale eye, mist and dawn light, mirrored reflection,
> fantasy wildlife illustration, painterly game avatar, circular portrait,
> highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

## 1450 — between the river and the fire

Between **Marlin** (1400, blue-and-silver fish spirit) and **Ember** (1500, fox
of fire). Club tier. The palette has to turn here — cold to warm — or Ember
arrives out of nowhere.

**Option A — Lynx**
A forest lynx, tufted ears, frost still on its ruff, standing where the water
ends and the woods begin. Warm greys and rust: the first warm coat on the
ladder, one rung before the fire.

> Powerful forest lynx spirit, tufted black ear tips, thick rust and grey
> ruff, frost melting on fur, standing at a riverbank at dusk, piercing
> golden eyes, elegant fantasy wildlife illustration, painterly game avatar,
> circular portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Vesper**
A creature of the hour between: a great dusk moth spirit, wings of ash with
ember-orange eyespots, drawn to a light that is not shown.

> Giant dusk moth spirit, ash-grey wings with glowing ember-orange eyespots,
> feathered antennae, warm smoke and firelight palette, twilight forest
> background, mystical fantasy portrait, painterly game avatar, circular
> portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

## 1600 — between the stone and the gem

Between **Basalt** (1550, moss-armoured rock giant) and **Onyx** (1650, black
crystal raven). Club tier. Basalt is rough stone; Onyx is cut gemstone. This is
where the rock starts to take an edge.

**Option A — Cairn**
A sentinel of stacked stones held together by nothing visible, moss in the
joints, rune-cut faces, each block hovering a hand's width from the next. Still,
where Basalt is massive.

> Guardian of stacked floating stones held apart by glowing runes, moss and
> lichen in the gaps, single carved eye-slit, cold grey and pale green
> palette, ancient forest clearing, powerful nature guardian, painterly
> fantasy avatar, circular portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

**Option B — Flint**
Dark stone struck to a sharp edge, sparks caught along the fracture lines, the
first glint of gemstone in a body that is still rock. Leads straight into Onyx.

> Dark flint stone guardian with freshly fractured razor edges, orange sparks
> caught along the fracture lines, glowing amber core seen through cracks,
> charcoal and ember palette, ancient forest, powerful nature guardian,
> painterly fantasy avatar, circular portrait, highly detailed, 400x400 avatar

|---|---|---|---|

| 1050 | Casual | Bramble 1000 → Willow 1100 | Bracken / Juniper |
| 1150 | Casual | Willow 1100 → Vireo 1200 | Rowan / Aspen |
| 1350 | Casual (or Club) | Kestrel 1300 → Marlin 1400 | Osprey / Heron |
| 1450 | Club | Marlin 1400 → Ember 1500 | Lynx / Vesper |
| 1600 | Club | Basalt 1550 → Onyx 1650 | Cairn / Flint |

Every name is one word, a real thing in a wood, and unused — which is the whole
naming rule the existing twenty follow. Ids would be the lowercase of each.

## Two things to settle before any of this is built

**The unlock ladder doubles per roster slot.** `unlockCost` is
`1 << (index + 1)`, so inserting an opponent doubles the price of everyone above
them. Six insertions spread through the roster take Quadrim Prime from 1,048,576
points to 67,108,864, and every opponent above an insertion inherits a share of
that. Whatever these six are called, the cost curve is a separate decision and
it is the larger one: **six new rungs make the climb feel smoother only if the
prices do not make it four times longer.** The rung at 950 is exempt — it is
free — which is part of why it is the most valuable of the six.

**The Casual tier has to be re-measured.** 1050, 1150 and 1350 land inside the
band ticket 044 has only just straightened out (946, 1137, 1236, 1261, 1275
against their published anchors). New configurations in that band are new
chances for the kind of intransitive triangle that took three decision entries
to find. `calibrate.dart` plays every within-tier pair now, which is what would
catch it — but it has to actually be run, per new opponent, before any of these
ratings can be published.
