# 016 — First closed-test upload and tester recruitment

**What to build:** A real build of Quadrim is on Google Play's closed testing track, with twelve testers on it, and the fourteen-day clock is running.

This is the longest lead time in the project and it is not a programming task. Google Play requires personal developer accounts created after November 2023 to run a closed test with at least twelve testers for fourteen continuous days before production access is granted. The clock cannot start until a build is uploaded, and no amount of later effort recovers time not spent waiting.

Recruiting twelve people who will actually install and open the app is a real task with a real lead time. It is listed here as acceptance criteria rather than assumed, because it is the part most likely to be treated as a formality and then to block the release.

**Blocked by:** 011 — Opponent selection; 013 — Main screen; 015 — Store accounts and release signing

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A signed build is uploaded to the closed testing track
- [ ] The Data Safety declaration is completed — with no accounts, no network and no analytics, it declares no data collection
- [ ] The content rating questionnaire is completed
- [ ] A minimal store listing exists with title, short description, full description, icon and screenshots
- [ ] Twelve testers are recruited and have opted into the closed test
- [ ] All twelve have installed the app and opened it at least once
- [ ] The fourteen-day continuous-testing clock has started, and its start date is recorded
- [ ] A route exists for testers to report problems, and it is communicated to them
- [ ] Tester feedback is collected somewhere durable rather than in scattered messages
