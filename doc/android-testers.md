# Quadrim for Android — testers wanted

Quadrim for Android is finished, but it is not on Google Play yet.

Google requires a new developer account to run a **closed test with at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 days in a row**, before an app may be published to everyone. Until enough people have joined that test, there is no public Play Store listing to link to.

## How to help

1. Fill in the form at the bottom of **[evict.nl/quadrim](https://evict.nl/quadrim)** with the email address of your Google account.
2. You will receive an invitation link to the test on Google Play.
3. Install Quadrim from that link and keep it installed for at least 14 days. Playing a game now and then and leaving feedback helps too.

The test version is the full game: thirty-one opponents, pass and play, quests and the tutorial.

Once Quadrim has passed review it will be published on Google Play and this page will link straight to it.
